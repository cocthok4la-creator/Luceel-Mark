**Luceel Mark**

A free QR code generator. Make a QR code, style it, and download it in seconds. No signup, no watermark.

Website: https://luceel-mark-qr.me/

**Features**

- 11 QR types: URL, text, email, phone, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, UPI, contact card, social link, location, calendar event
- Custom colors, dot styles, gradients, and your own logo
- Download as PNG, SVG, or PDF
- Print-ready templates: poster, table tent, business cards, sticker sheet
- Your QR content never leaves your browser
